Zomato is an Indian multinational company offering online food ordering and delivery, restaurant discovery, and dining-out services, founded in 2008 by Deepinder Goyal and Pankaj Chaddah. It evolved from a restaurant listing site, Foodiebay, to a comprehensive food tech ecosystem connecting customers, restaurants, and delivery partners. Zomato operates in numerous cities and countries, providing services like online ordering, table reservations, and Zomato Pro (now a customer membership program). The company also engages in business-to-business (B2B) supplies through Hyperpure and offers grocery delivery via its acquisition of Blinkit.
